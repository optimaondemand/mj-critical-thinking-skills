📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Goal Hacking — Work Backward to Move Forward
Ever watch a heist movie where the crew pulls off an impossible mission — like cracking a vault and escaping undetected? The most thrilling part isn't the action — it's the plan. The mastermind always starts with the end: walking away clean with the prize. Then, they work backward, piece by piece, until they know exactly what needs to happen first.

Planning your goals can work the same way. You start at your big win — whatever success looks like for you — and think backward step by step until you reach today. It's called reverse-engineering, and once you learn how to do it, you'll be the mastermind of your own goals.

🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to use reverse-engineering to plan a real goal step by step backward from success.
2
I will be able to spot obstacles at each step and plan how to handle them.

🧠 REVERSE-ENGINEERING EXPLAINED

Reverse-engineering means starting at your goal and thinking backwards. Follow these steps to build your personal roadmap.

🪄 Step 1: Imagine the End Clearly

Picture what success looks like. Be specific.

Example: "Leave the casino with the money safely and undetected."

🧩 Step 2: Identify the Final Step

What is the last thing you'll do before you reach your goal?

Example: "Sneak out through the service tunnels with the duffel bags full of cash."

🔙 Step 3: Work Backward One Step at a Time

Ask: "What happens just before that?" Repeat this question until you reach today.

Example: "Enter the vault; before that create a distraction to enter the code to get into the vault; before that figure out the code to get into the vault; before that… before that — today — decide I want to break into a casino vault."

🔁 Step 4: Flip It Forward

Now put your steps in the right order — from first to last. This is your roadmap to success!

⚠️ WORTH KNOWING · AI AS A REVERSE-ENGINEERING PARTNER
Stuck on "What happens just before that?" Asking an AI to brainstorm intermediate steps for a goal you've described can speed up the rough draft of your roadmap. But check each step: AI tends to suggest plausible-sounding steps that may not match your specific situation. Use it to widen the field of options. Make the roadmap your own.
⚡ TRY IT · WHAT COMES JUST BEFORE?
For each goal, pick the action that most clearly comes immediately before reaching it.
1. Goal: Run a 5K race without stopping.
2. Goal: Get the lead role in the school musical.
3. Goal: Get an A on the unit exam.

Pick a new goal. Find a space with a mountain, pyramid, or tall sloped structure. Build a scene of you achieving the goal at the top. Then walk down, representing each prior step with props, sticky notes, and recorded walkthroughs. Record a video narrating the climb up.
💪 THE TAKEAWAY

Reverse-engineering isn't just a planning tool — it's a way of thinking. When you learn to break big goals into clear, realistic steps, you take control of your path forward. Instead of guessing what might work, you're building a strategy that starts from success and leads directly back to today. Whether your dream is creative, academic, or athletic, this method helps you move forward with confidence and clarity — one step at a time.
