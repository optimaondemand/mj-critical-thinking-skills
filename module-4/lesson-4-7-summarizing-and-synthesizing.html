📚 PART 1 · WHAT IS SUMMARIZING?

Summarizing is when you take a text and shrink it to its most important points, using your own words. You're not copying. You're rephrasing the key ideas in a way that makes sense to you.

🧭 The Friend Test: Imagine your friend asks, "What was that article about?" Your answer — 2 to 3 sentences long — is your summary. If your friend understands the main point, you nailed it.

How to summarize, step-by-step:

  1. Read the full text.
  2. Highlight or jot down the most important ideas.
  3. Ignore minor details and extra examples.
  4. Put the ideas into your own words.
  5. Keep it short (1-3 sentences for a paragraph or section).
📚 PART 2 · WHAT IS SYNTHESIZING?

Synthesizing means combining ideas from multiple texts to form a new understanding. It's not just summarizing each source — it's comparing, contrasting, and building your own conclusion.

Example: You read three articles —
1. One says screen time hurts sleep.
2. One says games improve reaction time.
3. One says online games help people make friends.

A synthesis might be: "Games have benefits and drawbacks — they help people think quickly and socialize, but may harm sleep if overused."

How to synthesize, step-by-step:

  1. Read at least two texts on the same topic.
  2. Take notes on the key ideas in each.
  3. Find connections — agreement, disagreement, different perspectives.
  4. Group similar ideas together.
  5. Make your own conclusion or insight.
  6. Explain it clearly in your own words.
⚡ TRY IT · SUMMARY OR SYNTHESIS?
Which skill is each description?
"I took three articles about video games and drew my own conclusion."
"I explained a long article about bees in three short sentences."
"I combined evidence from a science paper and a news article to argue for a conclusion neither one made alone."
📚 PART 3 · SUMMARY vs. SYNTHESIS — KNOW THE DIFFERENCE
Skill What it means When to use it
Summarizing Shrinks one text into its key points After reading an article, chapter, or lecture
Synthesizing Combines multiple texts into a new conclusion When writing essays, comparing sources, forming an opinion
⚠️ WORTH KNOWING · AI SUMMARIZES, BUT YOU SYNTHESIZE
AI tools are good at summarizing — they can take a long article and produce a short one. They're WORSE at real synthesis, because synthesis requires understanding what each source means, where they agree and disagree, and what NEW idea emerges when you put them together. That's the move only a human reader can really do well. When you build the Connection Web in your VR library next, you're doing exactly the work AI struggles to replace.
